First Principle Proposals
- Domain Specific Deterministic Algorithms Outperform Probabilistic Models
- Entities Possess Identifiable Features That Distinguish Them From Other Entities
- Memories are Events Experienced by an Entity, From its Perspective
- An Entity's Behavior is Influenced by its Experienced Events

Tenet Proposals
- Memory is experience, not context.
	NPCFramework models memory as structured events that happened to an NPC rather than as unexamined conversation history, blobs of information, or embeddings
