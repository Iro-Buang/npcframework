NPCFramework First Principles

A. of Compute, Costs, and Hardware Constraints
	- Compute Costs Resources
	- Latency Exists
	- No system is perfect
	- Data must live somewhere


B. Of Behavior, Algorithm, and System Design
	- Models Hallucinate
	- Context is finite
	- Environments Vary
	- Systems Evolve Over Time
	- No system has Complete Context


Tenets
	- NPCs Are Defined by Identity, Persona, Memory, State, Perception, Decision Making Kernel, and Actions.
	- Models and Behavioral Frameworks are commodities
	- Inference and Compute Resources are Utilities.
	- All Game Engines, Applications, and Environments that have interactivity have Universal Common Denominators.
	- Memory and User Data are Sacred and Private, with the user/creator of memory assuming full exclusive rights and control, everyone else shall be opt-in	
	- I/O must follow a coherent universal pipeline
	- NPC instances must be portable across environments, sessions, and engines
	- Tools/actions must follow a standardized schema.
	- Opinions and defaults are presets, optionality, modularity and open design are mandates
	- User Experience and Ease of Use is more important than backend sophistication and elegance.
